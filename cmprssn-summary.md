## What It Is
CMPRSSN is an organizational consulting firm focused on **architecting organizations for the agentic era**. Its thesis: "AI is only as capable as the *organization* around it." While AI capability advances fast, most companies are held back by a **structural ceiling** — outdated org design, roles, and governance — that prevents AI from delivering real value.

## The Problem They're Solving
- **55%** of AI projects never reach production (RAND)
- **80%** of workplace AI use runs through personal accounts, creating security/compliance risk (Cyberhaven)
- **41%** of companies abandoned most AI initiatives this year (S&P Global)

The gap isn't the models — it's the organizations trying to adopt them.

## Methodology — Three Phases

1. **Diagnose** — Proprietary assessment of AI maturity across three dimensions: people, organization, and technology.
2. **Redesign** — Restructure roles, teams, and governance; retool the technical stack; rebuild operating models around organizational intelligence.
3. **Deploy** — Execute via specialized partner teams (infrastructure, governance, security, compliance) rather than locking clients into a single vendor.
